Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Matveyev Nicolay, Romania, trading as Darkify ("we"), collects when you visit darkify.xyz or buy a Kit, and what your rights are. We are the data controller for the data described here. Contact: support@darkify.xyz.

1. Visiting the Site

The Site is a static website. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), processes your IP address and basic request data (browser, time, page) to deliver the Site and protect it from abuse. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

2. Buying a Kit

Purchases are handled by Paddle.com, our reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle collects your name, email address, billing country/address and payment details, and processes them under its own privacy policy. We never see or store your full card details. Paddle shares with us your name, email address, country, and the details of your order so we can deliver the Kit, send updates and provide support.

When you click "Buy", Paddle's checkout loads from Paddle's servers and may use cookies needed for the payment to work and to prevent fraud.

3. Contacting us

If you email us, we process your email address and message to answer you.

4. Why we use your data (legal basis)

  • Delivering your Kit, updates and support: performance of a contract.
  • Keeping order and tax records: legal obligation.
  • Preventing fraud and abuse: legitimate interests.

We do not send marketing emails and we do not sell or rent your personal data.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as tax and accounting law requires (typically up to 10 years), and support emails for up to 2 years after your last message.

6. Who receives your data

Paddle (payments and order handling), GitHub (website hosting) and our email provider (support messages). Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area under appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your data, and to data portability. To use these rights, email support@darkify.xyz. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
