FAQ

Which distributions are supported?

The kits are built and tested on Arch Linux and Arch-based distributions (such as Omarchy, EndeavourOS and CachyOS). Most files work on other distributions, but the installer assumes pacman.

Will it overwrite my current setup?

No. The installer copies your existing files to ~/.tidyrig-backup/<date> before it changes anything, and ./install.sh --restore puts them back.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once per kit. That includes 12 months of updates, and you keep the files forever.
